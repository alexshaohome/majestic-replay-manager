Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Overview

Majestic Replay Manager is a personal Windows desktop application that automates the management of gameplay recordings on the owner's YouTube channel.

2. Information Accessed

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access the authorized user's YouTube account. It accesses video identifiers, metadata, privacy status, and information necessary to upload and manage videos.

3. How Information Is Used

YouTube account access is used exclusively to upload recordings, check their privacy status, and delete expired private videos previously uploaded by this application.

4. Data Storage

Application settings, OAuth credentials, upload history, and video identifiers are stored locally on the user's computer. OAuth credentials are protected using Windows security mechanisms.

5. Data Sharing

The application does not sell or share personal information with third parties. Video data is transferred to YouTube using Google's official API.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

The application retains local metadata for operational purposes and can automatically delete uploaded private videos according to the configured retention period. Local recordings may also be deleted according to user-configured settings.

7. Revoking Access

Users can revoke the application's Google account permissions at Google Account Connections .
