<h1>Majestic Replay Manager</h1>

<p>
  A personal Windows desktop application for automatically
  managing gameplay recordings on YouTube.
</p>

<h2>Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Automatic detection of new gameplay recordings</li>
  <li>Private video uploads using the YouTube Data API</li>
  <li>Configurable video retention and cleanup</li>
  <li>Automatic cleanup of local recordings</li>
  <li>Upload history and video management</li>
</ul>

<h2>Google Account Access</h2>
<p>
  The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access the
  authorized user's YouTube channel for uploading and
  managing gameplay recordings.
</p>
